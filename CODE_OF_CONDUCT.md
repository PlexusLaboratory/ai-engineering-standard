# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim for a professional, inclusive, evidence-based collaboration space. Treat people with respect, critique ideas and work rather than people, and make room for different levels of experience and communication styles.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and honest about technical concerns.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and intellectual-property rights.
- Assume good faith while asking for clarification when intent is unclear.
- Accept feedback, correct mistakes, and help maintain a safe working environment.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in non-consensual professional contexts.
- Doxxing, publishing private information, or sharing credentials.
- Deliberate disruption, bad-faith reporting, or pressure to bypass security and safety controls.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, request changes, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect participants and the project. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer when a contact channel is available; do not expose affected people through public reports without their consent.

Project owners should add a named or private reporting channel before public launch.
