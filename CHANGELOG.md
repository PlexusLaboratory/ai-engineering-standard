## Release-note rules / Правила заметок о релизе

- Group entries under `Added`, `Changed`, `Deprecated`, `Removed`, `Fixed`, or `Security`.
- Describe user-visible behavior and migration impact, not internal commit activity.
- Link issues and pull requests when the repository has stable public URLs.
- Do not include secrets, private incidents, or exploit details.
